HOW TO PLAY

PLAYER 1
AD move · W jump · S block
F punch · G kick · H special · R SUPER
In 1-player mode the arrow keys + JKLI also work.
PLAYER 2
←→ move · ↑ jump · ↓ block
J punch · K kick · L special · I SUPER
MODES
Arcade: beat all 5 rivals for a high score (saved on this device). Training: infinite time, free meter, passive dummy. Gamepads work too (X punch, A kick, Y special, B/RB super).
ADVANCED
Double-tap ←/→ to dash (a back-dash is briefly invincible). Hold block + kick for a knockdown sweep. Perfect rounds give big bonus points. Frost's shards slow the enemy. Best of 3 rounds, 60s each. P/Esc pauses.
